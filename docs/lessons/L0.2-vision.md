# Product vision: Breast Pathology AI Platform

*Lesson L0.2 deliverable. Copy to `docs/product/vision.md` and make it your own.*
*Version 0.1 · 2026-10 · Owner: Mohtashim*

## Problem

Breast pathologists need a way to find cancer in slides quickly and grade it consistently, because searching gigapixel slides by eye is slow and tiring, and grading is subjective, which causes missed small metastases, disagreement between pathologists on grade, and long turnaround times as case volumes grow faster than the number of pathologists.

## Vision

A research platform that takes digitized breast pathology slides from any scanner, checks their quality, and shows pathologists where the AI sees cancer and what grade it estimates, together with how certain it is, so that pathologists can review faster and more consistently while always making the final decision.

## Intended use

- Research and **decision support** only. The pathologist always makes the diagnosis.
- Not a medical device; not cleared for primary diagnosis.

## Users and their jobs

| User | Job to be done | Top pain today |
|---|---|---|
| Pathologist | Sign out each case correctly and on time | Searching many lymph node slides for small metastases; inconsistent grading |
| Lab manager | Meet turnaround targets without extra staff | No visibility of backlog and where slides wait |
| Lab technician | Get good scans through quickly | Bad scans are found late, by the pathologist |
| Data scientist | Train, compare and release better models safely | No standard way to evaluate and deploy models |
| Administrator | Keep users, roles and data secure | Patient data in slide labels and logs |

Details: [pathologist interview](interviews/pathologist.md), [lab manager interview](interviews/lab-manager.md).

## What the product does (v1 to v3)

1. **v1, detection:** ingest slides, quality control, tumor heatmaps with uncertainty in a web viewer. Data: PatchCamelyon and CAMELYON16 (breast cancer lymph node metastases).
2. **v2, grading:** Nottingham grade estimate with uncertainty, using TCGA-BRCA slides with grades extracted from pathology reports; triage worklist and lab dashboard.
3. **v3, real data:** partner-lab slides and pathologist annotations, swapped in without code changes; deployed to the cloud.

## Product principles

- **Pathologist in control:** show where, how sure and why; easy to disagree.
- **Works on real data:** any common slide format; mpp stored per slide; robust to stain and scanner differences; datasets are configuration, not code.
- **Honest AI:** calibrated probabilities and uncertainty; "I don't know" is an allowed answer.
- **Privacy by design:** no patient identifiers in the platform; label images removed; every access audited.
- **Simple first:** the smallest version that delivers value, then improve.

## Success metrics

| Level | Metric | Target (v1) |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome | Review time per lymph node case (simulated) | −30% vs no AI |
| Outcome | Bad scans caught before pathologist review | ≥ 90% |
| Model | CAMELYON16 slide-level AUC | ≥ 0.90 |
| Model | Sensitivity at chosen operating point | ≥ 0.95 |
| Model | Calibration error (ECE) | ≤ 0.05 |
| Model (v2) | Grade agreement with pathologists (weighted kappa) | ≥ inter-pathologist agreement |
| Platform | Tile response time (p95) | < 200 ms |
| Platform | One slide processed end to end on CPU | < 10 min |

## Out of scope

Primary diagnosis without a pathologist; regulatory clearance; scanner control; billing and multi-tenant SaaS.

## Biggest risks

1. **Grade labels are noisy:** pathologists disagree; report-extracted grades may be missing or wrong.
2. **Domain shift:** a model trained on public data may fail on partner slides (stain, scanner, mpp).
3. **Scope:** one person, 32 weeks; every feature must earn its place.
4. **Data access:** partner data approvals can take 6+ months; start contacting labs early.
